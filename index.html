About me

I enjoy working across the entire stack, from low-level systems programming close to the hardware up to modern full-stack applications, databases, and virtualization. Understanding how every layer of a product connects end-to-end is what drives my curiosity.

Outside of lectures, I build with my hackathon team Elysa's Secret, run the Robotics Club at SDU Vejle, and spend time configuring servers, networking, and containers in homelab environments. Whether architecting a system or writing code at SDU, my focus is always on practical problem solving: building clean, maintainable, and production-ready software.

Outside of coding, I'm passionate about motorcycles and understanding how complex machines work—a mechanical curiosity that directly fuels my interest in robotics, embedded systems, and software that interacts with the physical world.
